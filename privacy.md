# Element-Hider Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Element-Hider allows you to customize your web experience by hiding unwanted elements.

## 1. Local Storage
Your custom CSS selectors and rules are stored locally in your browser. They are used only to apply visual changes to the websites you visit as per your configuration.

## 2. No Tracking
The extension does not track your browsing history or collect any data from the pages you visit.
